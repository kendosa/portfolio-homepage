Studio Glagola

Furniture built to outlast trends. A brand built the same way.

Studio Glagola makes custom furniture and cabinetry in the Hudson Valley. The work is precise, considered, and built to outlast trends. The brand needed to be the same.

I designed the identity end to end, and built a utilitarian logo suite around Neue Haas Grotesk and a monogram mark, then developed a type and image system flexible enough to show up seriously on a business card and loosely across social. Restraint at the core, range at the edges.

The work covers logo design, full brand identity, type system, and art direction across photography and layout.

On the street and on the screen

From Instagram to Upstate House

The identity was built to loosen up on social, and that’s exactly where this job came from. Matthew Goldman found Studio Glagola on Instagram and brought the shop in, with interior designer Samantha Leeds of Creatures of Place, to build The Common Good, a bookstore and bar in Ellenville. Its 27‑foot red oak bar, curved by hand on site, landed the shop in Upstate House, the Hudson Valley’s cultural hub for architecture and interior design.
