Endeavor Health

Four competing health systems.
One city. One overnight merger.

Endeavor Health launched in 2023 as one of the largest healthcare systems in the Chicago area, built from institutions with decades of separate histories. Working with TRIPTK, I helped build the brand identity tasked with making it feel like it had always been one.

As a design director on the project, I owned [the visual system, the campaign line system, and wayfinding]. The challenge was giving four institutions with their own loyal patients one voice, without any of them feeling absorbed by the others.

One system from four

Four institutions, each with its own loyal patients, needed one mark that none of them could claim. Dozens of iterations later, the team landed on a monogram E built from three stacked strokes, steady enough to anchor a hospital system and enough of a sense of movement to feel like it’s headed somewhere. Paired with ES Klarheit Grotesk by Extraset, it’s a quiet core that lets the campaign do the talking.

A line everyone can finish

Every line in the campaign ends the same way. Your best health is our endeavor. [Your recovery is our endeavor. Your first breath is our endeavor.] The blank belongs to the patient and the promise belongs to the system, so the line works on a billboard, a discharge folder, or a badge without ever sounding generic.

Where Chicago actually lives

Within a year of launch, Endeavor Health was showing up where Chicago actually lives. The logo appears on the ice at United Center during Blackhawks games, and in Season 3 of The Bear, in the hospital where Natalie gives birth. Not placed. Just present.
