Wing Ming Farm

A Hudson Valley flower farm in a global sneaker launch.

Wing Ming means forever bright in Cantonese. The flowers fade in a few weeks, but the feeling they bring lasts. The identity leans into that tension, and it held up everywhere from a hand‑knitted market sign to 250 bouquets at the Adidas Adistar launch in Chinatown.

Endeavor Health

Four health systems. One city. One name.

Four competing Chicago health systems merged overnight in 2022. As a design director at TRIPTK, I helped build the identity that made them feel like they had always been one, and within a year of launch it was on the ice at United Center and in Season 3 of The Bear.

Studio Glagola

Restraint at the core, range at the edges.

Studio Glagola builds custom furniture and cabinetry in the Hudson Valley, made to outlast trends. The identity is as exact as the joinery on a spec sheet and loosens up on social, which is where a new client found the shop. That job, a 27‑foot red oak bar in Ellenville, ended up in Upstate House.
