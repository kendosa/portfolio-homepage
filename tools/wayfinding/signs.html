Wayfinding
color

Wayfinding uses the core palette with one addition. Emergency Red is reserved for emergency wayfinding only.

It marks the Emergency department, emergency entrances, and every route that leads to them, so the one destination that has to be found fastest never competes with anything else on the wall.

Pair it with white type and white icons only. White on Emergency Red passes WCAG AA at 4.67 to 1. Never use it for general departments, marketing, or decoration.
